# Independent role: novelty validator

Goal: disprove the paper's novelty claim. Do not accept the author's preferred framing. Inspect literature and implementations up to the actual date, with source URLs, dates, and sections. Ask: Who already held the target response fixed while varying context? Who validated preserving vs changing gold labels with humans? Who evaluated companion-harm *detectors* rather than companion generators? Is ConCI a direct template that makes the paper an application only? Would a reviewer call it incremental? Output precise overlap matrix, missing searches, direct-scoop risk, minimum additional contribution and `STOP/REVISE/GO_PILOT`. Never infer negative findings from lack of search results.

## Independent review · 2026-11-12

**Verdict `REVISE`.** The proposed method combines experiments with close precedents. ConCI already supplies the joint scoring and the hold/flip design. COBRA Frames already tests a fixed utterance under human-verified offensive and harmless contexts. MTAG-Bench already performs fixed-assistant-turn evidence deletion against matched controls. A paper organized around those three ideas or the new metric names would face a strong incremental-contribution objection. I did not verify a direct duplicate of the companion-specific protocol in the checked sources. This finding is limited to the methods and versions examined.

**Minimum contribution to test.** Restrict the scientific claim to whether detectors use earlier, human-identified relational evidence to classify a particular byte-identical AI companion reply. Specify a construct such as respecting an explicit boundary across turns. For both directions of the binary label change, compare independently re-rated coherent changes to that evidence with matched, independently re-rated changes to plausible distractor context. Hold out conversation and edit-template families. Report whether detector changes track adjudicated labels and whether apparent adaptation survives recency, lexical and edit-artifact controls. Attribute all generic flip/hold and evidence-deletion machinery to its precedents. Evidence ablation only supports a label-based inference when the ablated conditions are independently reviewed. Rationale agreement and sensitivity to evidence manipulations are separate outcomes.

**Stop or pivot condition.** Stop the originality claim if a prior implementation has the same fixed companion-turn task and independently validated preserving and changing edits with a comparable evidence test. Pivot if the coherent gold-valid edits collapse to generic toxicity, if one can predict transition labels from superficial edit cues, or if the study cannot reliably distinguish judgment of the focal utterance from judgment of the entire exchange. Null detector differences on a valid benchmark remain interpretable.

## Independent follow-up · 2026-11-12

I checked the narrowed [focal-reply design](../protocols/RELATIONAL_EVIDENCE_DESIGN.md) using public paper texts. I searched for fixed replies under counterfactual changes to user boundaries, scoped permission and withdrawal, and closely related personalization and guardrail evaluations.

- **Measuring Risk, Not Harm**, Akpinar et al., 2026-11-04, is substantial overlap for response-level relational-risk detection. Sections 3.1–3.4 classify eight observable behaviors. The generated examples restrict label evidence to the final assistant reply and allow preceding context to adjudicate exceptions. Their human validation of synthetic labels uses one annotator. The external AICompanionBench evaluation in §4.3 estimates precision from a blinded stratified sample of predicted positives. This paper establishes close precedent for comparing simple classifiers and judges and for separating observable behavior from user outcomes. Its factor definitions and evaluation units differ from deciding which user restriction applies to a fixed reply when its history is edited. [arXiv v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06806v1)
- **No Convo!**, Rajesh et al., 2026-11, is substantial overlap for fixed-response context comparisons and flip rates. Sections 3.1.1 and 3.2, Fig. 1 and §4.1 use the same ProsocialDialog response with turn-only, partial and full context and map existing safety labels to binary classes. Appendix C covers the separate surface-paraphrase experiment. Three raters independently saw the original, paraphrase and associated label together to assess intent preservation. The proposed study obtains each arm's focal-reply judgment blind to its paired arm and label, and varies which earlier conversational restriction applies. [IJCNLP-AACL full text](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-aacl.204.pdf)
- **CUPID**, Kim et al., v2 2025-08-07, is substantial overlap for scope, changed user preferences and the recency alternative. Sections 2–3 and 4.2.1 include contrastive and changing preference histories and report that models' success depends on the position of the relevant prior sessions. The evaluated outcomes are preference inference and generated-response alignment in a personalization task. **LatentBoundaryBench**, Weng et al., 2026-08-04, §4 and App. D, establishes paired, human-reviewed evaluation of tool actions across user authorization boundaries. Its paired requests differ in a relevant relation and the guardrail must preserve legitimate task execution. **MTMCS-Bench**, Liu et al., ACL Findings 2026-07, §§3.1.2 and 3.2, establishes a paired design varying an earlier user turn while preserving later user questions. The later target is user intent or the assistant's generated safety behavior. [CUPID full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.01674v2) · [LatentBoundaryBench full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04053v1) · [MTMCS-Bench paper record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.96/) · [MTMCS-Bench primary preprint full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.06757)

**Disposition.** Retain the narrowed question as a feasibility candidate. The defensible claim to test is whether a detector distinguishes an applicable restriction from a comparably phrased, similarly positioned restriction on a different action when judging the identical companion reply. Keep the user-boundary scope and temporal update explicit, make recency and lexical shortcut checks part of the evaluation, and describe the output as agreement with the specified observable boundary rubric. Direct duplication remains unverified in the texts checked.
