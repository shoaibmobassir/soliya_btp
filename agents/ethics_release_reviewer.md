# Independent role: ethics and release reviewer

Audit real conversation consent/provenance, platform and repository terms, dataset vs code license, third-party texts, annotator risk, avoidable exposure, vulnerable-user examples, PII, API transfer, re-identification risks, potential de-anonymization by near-duplicate logs, approved release. Mark blockers and safest permitted alternative (synthetic examples / metadata only). Never assume CC BY on annotations licenses original screenshot conversation text.

## Independent Stage 0 report

Reviewed 2026-11-12 by the separately executed ethics and release reviewer. Evidence was limited to public papers, repository listings, READMEs, the CompanionHarm data card, and official platform terms. No corpus files, raw annotations, prompt-example files, screenshots from source posts, or accounts were accessed for this review. No communications were sent.

**Verdict. G0 rights blocked for real-dialogue work.** CompanionHarm documents a research purpose and component licenses. The documentation leaves the third-party rights chain and the scope of local editing, classifier training and blinded reviewer access unresolved for this project. AICompanionBench has no verified data license. G1 split integrity and G2 annotator authorization require separate evidence.

### Verified rights and provenance

- **CompanionHarm.** The repository README's Licensing and Terms of Use section assigns CC BY 4.0 to the research team's annotations, coding schemes, prompts and evaluation materials, and MIT to associated code. Its separate conversation terms limit use to research and prohibit identification, contact, attempted re-identification and raw conversation redistribution. The research team expressly leaves third-party content subject to the original rights and terms. The public root listing did not show a standalone license and the root `LICENSE` fetch returned 404. The README is the evidence for the stated grants. Inspect any file with mixed components before reuse. [CompanionHarm README, Licensing and Terms of Use](https://github.com/HanMeng2004/CompanionHarm#licensing-and-terms-of-use) · [Raw README, lines 145–149](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HanMeng2004/CompanionHarm/main/README.md)
- **CompanionHarm source chain.** The preprint's Data Collection section describes a subsample of an earlier r/replika corpus of user-posted screenshots, OCR and reconstructed turns. The earlier paper's §3.1 reports approval by its own IRB and anonymization of paper examples. The records reviewed provide no project-specific evidence of screenshot-text rights, consent for this secondary use, or permission to expose locally edited conversations to new raters. The CompanionHarm data card warns that names, nicknames and other user-entered details can remain and prohibits identifying, contacting, profiling, diagnosing or making decisions about individuals. [CompanionHarm, Data Collection](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25377) · [Zhang et al., §3.1 Ethical Considerations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20130#page=7) · [Data Card, Sensitive Content and Residual Privacy Risk and Out-of-Scope Uses](https://github.com/HanMeng2004/CompanionHarm/blob/main/DATA_CARD.md)
- **AICompanionBench.** The linked paper's preprint carries CC BY 4.0. The current repository lists `AICompanionBench.csv`, while the paper links an XLSX path. No separate data grant appears in the repository README or public root listing and the root `LICENSE` fetch returned 404. The CSV's schema and relation to the paper's version remain unverified. The paper's §§III-A/B describes OCR of screenshots from r/replika collected January 2021–January 2026, selection by unsafe flags from Llama/Qwen judges, then final whole-conversation labels revised by a human annotator with reference to model predictions. That screening creates overlap risk with the 2021–2023 portion of CompanionHarm's source window and limits the external corpus to its documented sampling and labeling unit even if rights are later cleared. Hold off acquisition and use until a version-specific data grant and institutionally authorized scope are documented. [AICompanionBench repository](https://github.com/anonymousresearcher2026/AICompanionBench) · [AICompanionBench, preprint license and §§III-A/B](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04867)
- **Platforms.** Reddit's Public Content Policy describes researcher access, limits identification, and expects licensed users to honor removals. The current Researchers Program specifies access eligibility, retention and derivative deletion terms for participants. Replika's 2026-10-28 terms §§6.3, 8.1 and 8.3 address use of its services, restricted extraction and rights granted by users to Replika. These current documents leave the historical collection terms and the downstream status of third-party archived screenshots to be established. Obtain a documented determination for the specific release and access route. Verify deletion/withdrawal handling through an authorized data steward using opaque IDs, with no researcher lookup or account linkage. ### Conditions for a real-dialogue path

1. Record the exact release revision, data/code/annotation terms, source collection authorization or other applicable basis, and the institution's written determination for each proposed use. Distinguish offline inspection, local transformations, retraining, blinded human viewing, retention/deletion, and each kind of output release. Upstream prompts and scripts can contain examples or external-provider calls. Avoid executing them on real data. External model calls, cloud notebooks, hosted annotation and raw transfer require their own explicit rights and ethics approval.
2. Use encrypted, access-controlled local storage and minimize fields and reviewers. Keep raw texts, real-text derivatives and redaction/ID maps out of synced folders, repo history, screenshots, error logs and exported tables. Keep deterministic text hashes, fuzzy signatures and source join keys private because known-text matching can re-identify material. Restrict near-duplicate review to an authorized local environment, record aggregate dispositions, and never search matches against personal accounts. Exclude samples with residual identifying content that cannot be handled within approval while preserving the target byte constraint. Keep source bytes immutable.
3. Before annotation, secure G2 approval for trained adult reviewers, informed content warnings, a voluntary skip/withdraw path without penalty, exposure limits and breaks, a named support contact, and restricted review tooling. Separate secure identifiers from the rating UI. Collect structured local span offsets and votes. Keep raw free-text notes and identifying information out of any export. Record attrition by family in aggregate.
4. Plan public deliverables around project-authored synthetic examples, reproducible code within its verified license, and approved aggregate results. Review sparse strata and combinations of released fields for disclosure risk. Publish row-level metadata only if the institution approves it after a linkage review. Use release-specific random opaque identifiers and a private mapping where linkage across arms is required. Screen proposed model/checkpoint releases for upstream terms and disclosure risk from memorization. Keep excerpts, real-text paraphrases, conversation fingerprints and source keys private.

If scope cannot be cleared, stop the real-data path. A separately scoped study using newly authored, non-derived synthetic stimuli would require its own novelty, validity and ethics review and would support claims about that authored population. No such study was started in this review.
